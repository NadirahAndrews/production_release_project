2D Game Design

Wandering Whiskers is a Unity 2D game tailored to children aged 5-8, designed to foster creativity and problem-solving skills. Players guide a curious cat through a vibrant world, solving puzzles and collecting items to progress. The game emphasizes exploration and learning, with colorful graphics and engaging gameplay that encourages young minds to think critically while having fun.
