About Me

Hi, I'm a 3rd-year Digital Arts student at the University of the Witwatersrand, currently pursuing my creative passions through web development, design and animation.

I'm excitedly at the beginnings of my journey in animation and interactive media studies with a background in game design and development. I have a passion for storytelling and visual communication, and I'm eager to explore how these mediums can be used to create engaging and immersive experiences. This all stems for my love for the arts from a humble "doodling" beginning and a curiosity as to how my passions could be integrated into a modern world.

My interests lie in the cross section of art and technology, and while I pursue this interest, I remain open to exploring all kinds of creative opportunities. In a world of generative AI and advanced technologies, I am centred on the continuation of artistic expression and human creativity. I am fascinated by the potential of these technologies to enhance and augment human creativity, and I am excited to see how they will shape the future of art and design.

I am eager for learning opportunities and collaborations that will allow me to grow as an artist and a creative professional. I am excited to see where my journey in animation and interactive media studies will take me, and I am committed to using my skills and creativity to make a positive impact in the world.
